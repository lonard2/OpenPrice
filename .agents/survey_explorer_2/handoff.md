## 3. Caveats

1. **Live OpenRouter API Key Availability:**
   - `process.env.OPENROUTER_API_KEY` may not always be configured in all local developer environments. A deterministic local heuristic fallback parser (with pre-mapped bounding boxes for sample shelf tags, flyers, and receipts) is required to ensure 100% test and demo reliability.
2. **Extreme Low-Sample Variance:**
   - For products with fewer than 3 historical price points ($N < 3$), sample standard deviation is statistically unreliable. The anomaly detector will fall back to percentage swing thresholds ($>50\%$ jump) until $N \ge 3$ verified points are accumulated.
